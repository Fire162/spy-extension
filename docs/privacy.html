LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy for Spy Extension

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. Overview

Spy Extension is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines our data handling practices for the Spy Extension Chrome Extension and the Web Controller client.

2. Data Collection & Storage

We collect zero personal data. Specifically:

  • No browsing history, URLs, or cookies are logged, collected, or uploaded.
  • No keystrokes, passwords, or personal credentials are stored.
  • No tracking cookies or telemetry analytics exist in the software.

3. Peer-to-Peer Transmission

All video streaming and remote input events occur directly between the host and remote controller via encrypted WebRTC peer-to-peer connections. Video data is processed ephemerally in volatile memory and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded to any third-party server.

4. Two-Party Consent

Remote browser access cannot be initiated automatically or covertly. Connection requests require the host to explicitly grant authorization via the extension interface.
